Notes now accept idea, model and spec alongside fact, term and guide. These distinguish motivation, domain structure and expected behavior without turning knowledge into delivery Tasks. Decisions retain rule, shape and drift: constraints, chosen designs and deliberate departures already cover their roles. Note kinds do not encode approval or implementation; bodies record those distinctions and unresolved choices remain Questions. Origins preserve how work arose, mentions carry supporting context and Task blocks express prerequisites. Older binaries must be upgraded before reading the new kinds.
