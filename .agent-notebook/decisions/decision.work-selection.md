Work views, including Recall and its startup hook, honor the same configured audience. Explicit audience flags override the configuration; mine narrows work without hiding shared knowledge during recall. Knowledge-only listings remain shared unless the caller explicitly narrows authorship. A filter narrows the answer, not the graph used to establish readiness. The for filter selects a record and its transitive origin descendants: composition does not include external prerequisites or contextual links. Graph focus and focused recall use the broader relationship neighbourhood for context. These distinctions keep ownership, readiness and relevant knowledge from changing meaning between commands.
