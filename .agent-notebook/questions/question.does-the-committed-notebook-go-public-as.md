The sweep removes the owner's internal information from the book, the README, the skills and the scripts. The notebook under .agent-notebook is committed by choice and will be public too: every record carries the owner's name in its by line, the task bodies carry dated rulings and the marathon's course, and the report Notes name the models that wrote and checked the code. Dogfooding is the point, and the notebook is honest history; the question is whether the owner accepts it public as it is, wants the by lines or the report Notes stripped before the repository opens, or keeps the notebook out of the public tree. Only the owner can answer.
