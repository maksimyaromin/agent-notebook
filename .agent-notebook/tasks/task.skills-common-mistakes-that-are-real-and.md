Owner ruling (2026-09-05) on the two public skills. First, the Common mistakes tables are banalities pulled in for symmetry; each row must be a mistake an agent actually makes with this tool because the tool's behaviour makes it easy, with the consequence and the move that avoids it, and nothing that a reader of the section above would never have done. Second, the page a fresh session drew by the atlas skill was overloaded around the map: a rail of counters, a header of controls, a footer of provenance, a panel opening on twenty envelope rows; the reference page had one narrow column (find, the legends that filter, the arrangement) and the map. The skill must prescribe that restraint, say when the ranked layout applies (one branch, never the whole notebook, which ranks into a rope), require names visible at the opening zoom, and let the slice follow the question (the archive is not part of a working question). docs/guides/atlas.md follows the skill.
